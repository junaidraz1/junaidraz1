## 🌱 What am I up to?
Right now I'm focused on **applied AI for clinical data**, with a deep dive into:
- **LLM-based clinical text classification** – running locally hosted models (Gemma-class LLMs) over Finnish-language patient notes, lab results, and nursing documentation, and turning them into structured, evidence-backed JSON output. 🧠
- **Rigorous evaluation of AI pipelines** – precision/recall/F1 by class, confusion matrices, ablations, and human-in-the-loop verification, because a model that "seems right" isn't good enough for health data. 📊
- **Biosignals & health wearables** – still fluent in EEG/ECG, and still making sure step counters don't lie about my laziness. 🚶‍♂️
